YouTube account

Checking

Signing in makes Home and radio follow your YouTube Music taste. On a computer, open music.youtube.com while logged in, press F12 → Network, click any browse request and copy its request headers (or just the Cookie value), then paste here. Playback itself stays anonymous.

People

Everyone picks their name once per device, so the queue shows who added what.

A name can have its own pass phrase, asked once per device. Removing a name, or taking off a forgotten pass phrase, needs the admin password.

Backup

Everything the house made in one file: people and their groups, playlists and likes, history, stats and settings. Pass phrases are in it only when the admin downloads it. Your YouTube sign-in is never in it, and neither is the audio of local songs: the admin's full backup has that.

Download a backup

A restore replaces all of that with the file's, and needs the admin password. What's here now is saved first, in the backups folder on the server.
